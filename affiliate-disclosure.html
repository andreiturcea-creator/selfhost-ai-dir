Last updated 15 October 2026.

SelfHostAI is free to use. To help pay for it, some links may be affiliate or referral links. If you click one and sign up or buy, we may earn a commission or credit, at no extra cost to you.

How we label them

  • Every affiliate or referral link is marked Affiliate link next to the link itself, not only on this page.
  • These links carry rel="sponsored" so search engines know they are paid links.
  • Featured listings. If we sell featured listings in future, they will be labelled 'Featured' or 'Sponsored'.

What it does not change

Commissions never change calculator results, figures, rankings or what we write. Tool details come from each project's public GitHub repository. We only link to services we think are relevant, and you can always go to a provider directly instead.

Current partners

We list partner links only once a programme has approved us. Right now there are none.

This follows the UK CAP Code and ASA guidance that marketing must be obviously identifiable. See also our privacy notice.
